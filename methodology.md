# Methodology

## Research Workflow
1. Selected Ahmedabad as the manufacturing hub.
2. Focused on specialty manufacturing segments.
3. Collected companies from:
   - Google
   - LinkedIn
   - Industry directories
   - Company websites
4. Evaluated companies using DT's Federer framework.

## Verification Process
- Verified manufacturing capability.
- Checked leadership backgrounds.
- Reviewed facility announcements.
- Looked for certifications and export activity.
- Cross-verified claims using multiple sources.

## AI Usage
AI was used for:
- Initial research structuring
- Categorization
- Workflow planning

Human review was used for:
- Final scoring
- Evidence validation
- Removing hallucinated claims
