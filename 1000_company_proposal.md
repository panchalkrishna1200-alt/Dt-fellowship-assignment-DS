# 1000 Company Proposal

## Goal
Build a verified database of 1000 ICP-qualified companies in one month.

## Funnel
4000 raw companies
↓
2500 screened
↓
1400 AI-qualified
↓
1000 human-verified

## Weekly Plan

### Week 1
Build raw database from:
- DSIR
- MCA
- Expo directories
- LinkedIn

### Week 2
Run AI-assisted scoring and classification.

### Week 3
Perform QA checks and remove false positives.

### Week 4
Finalize verified list and add personalization hooks.

## Quality Control
- Manual review for borderline companies
- Cross-verification of certifications
- Revenue range validation
- Leadership background verification
